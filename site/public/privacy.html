Privacy policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

The short version

Hodeum works on your PC without an account. If you sign in with Google, your learning progress syncs to your Hodeum account so you can see it on the web and on your other PCs. Screenshots, audio and voice transcripts never leave your PC.

What stays on your PC

To guide you, Hodeum reads the app you're working in (through Windows accessibility and, when needed, a screenshot) and listens when you talk to Hodey. This happens on your PC with local AI models. Screenshots and audio are processed in memory, never uploaded and never saved.

What we collect when you sign in

  • From Google: your name, email address and Google account ID, used only to sign you in. Hodeum asks for no other Google data.
  • Your learning data: skills and how much help you need for each, your Hodes (the goal, steps, hints and mistakes, and start and end times), your chats with Hodey, and your Hodeum settings.
  • Your PCs: each signed-in PC's name, when it was last online, and the Hode it's running, so the dashboard can show your PCs and start a Hode on one.

Signed-out use collects nothing. You can pause sync on any PC in Settings › Account.

How it's used and stored

Your data is used only to show you your progress and to keep your PCs in step. It's stored with Supabase (in the United States) and protected so that only your account can read or change it. We don't sell it, share it, use it for advertising or train AI models on it.

Optional web search

If you turn on web search in the app, Hodey sends a short, generic query (with emails, links, file names and long numbers removed) to Stack Exchange and Microsoft Learn. Your screen, files and Hodey's replies are never sent.

Deleting your data

Sign out to stop syncing. To delete your account and everything synced to it, email anshraj65@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and it will be deleted within 30 days. Data on your PC stays until you uninstall Hodeum.
