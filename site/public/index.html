AI that teaches,
not takes over.

Hodeum is a learning companion for your PC. Ask to learn something, like making a PivotTable in Excel, and Hodey guides you from a small notch at the top of your screen: it explains each step, highlights where to click, checks your work, and steps back as you get better. You stay in control and do the work yourself.

Learn by doing

Each guided journey, a Hode, happens in your real apps. Hodey points, you click. Make a mistake and it shows you how to recover.

Local first

Screen understanding and voice run on your PC. Screenshots and audio are never uploaded or saved.
